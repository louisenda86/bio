How to write a book

Write a little every day, finish a rough draft, then fix it in passes.

Guide

  1. Pin the ideaWrite one sentence: who the book is for and what changes for them by the last page.
  2. OutlineFiction: list the scenes that turn the story. Nonfiction: one chapter per part of the promise.
  3. Set a daily quota500 words a day gives a 50,000 word draft in about 100 days. Pick a fixed time and protect it.
  4. Draft to the endDo not edit while drafting. Leave gaps marked [TK] and keep moving until the last chapter is down.
  5. Rest the draftPut it away for two to four weeks. You will read it as a stranger would.
  6. Revise in passesStructure first (order, missing parts, cuts), then paragraphs, then sentences, then typos.
  7. Get readersGive it to three to five people with two or three specific questions, such as where they got bored.
  8. PublishQuery agents for a traditional deal, or self-publish with an editor, a cover and an ISBN.
